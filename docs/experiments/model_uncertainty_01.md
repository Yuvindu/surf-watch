# Confidence and Model Uncertainty 01

## Status and Objective

Completed on 7 October 2026 from clean revision
`f7ea315ffe822372dda05f204c35927424112cd4`. Measure the probability
reliability of SegFormer-B0, U-Net ResNet34, and their fixed 50/50 ensemble on
identical, source-aligned RipVIS validation frames. This is a descriptive
evaluation; no calibration transform, threshold, or ensemble weight was fitted.

## Pooled Probability Results

Scores pool pixels over all videos; lower Brier, NLL, and ECE are better.
Entropy describes prediction concentration and is not an accuracy score.

| Model path | All-pixel Brier | All-pixel NLL | All-pixel ECE | All-pixel entropy |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| SegFormer-B0 | 0.02728 | 0.13667 | 0.01864 | 0.03634 |
| U-Net ResNet34 | 0.03068 | 0.18959 | 0.02666 | 0.02303 |
| Equal-weight ensemble | **0.02581** | **0.12287** | **0.01787** | 0.03962 |

The common candidate region contains 676,862,481 pixels (6.92% of all pixels)
and includes every ground-truth positive pixel. Its foreground prevalence is
82.17%, versus 5.68% over all pixels. It is therefore intentionally enriched
and must not be interpreted as the deployment distribution.

| Model path | Candidate Brier | Candidate NLL | Candidate ECE | Candidate entropy |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| SegFormer-B0 | 0.37396 | 1.87428 | 0.35011 | 0.28360 |
| U-Net ResNet34 | 0.43609 | 2.70094 | 0.42033 | 0.23159 |
| Equal-weight ensemble | **0.36452** | **1.70965** | **0.31974** | 0.36880 |

The ensemble has the lowest pooled Brier, NLL, and ECE in both cohorts. The
all-pixel ECE advantage over SegFormer is small (0.01787 versus 0.01864), so
this run does not establish a generalisable calibration advantage. Moreover,
SegFormer has the lowest all-pixel Brier score on 19 of 36 individual videos,
the ensemble on 13, and U-Net on four. Pooled performance is not uniform
across videos.

These probability scores answer a different question from the earlier
[fixed-threshold segmentation evaluation](ensemble_fusion_01.md): SegFormer
had higher foreground IoU than the ensemble there (0.5027 versus 0.4701).
The current evidence therefore does not justify replacing SegFormer as the
default thresholded segmentation path merely because averaged probabilities
have lower pooled scoring-rule loss.

## Model Disagreement

Across all pixels, SegFormer and U-Net differ by a mean absolute foreground
probability of 0.02533 and disagree on the thresholded mask for 2.24% of
pixels. Within the common candidate region, those values rise to 0.26862 and
32.38%. This contrast is expected given the background-heavy image domain.

`RipVIS-NR-004` illustrates disagreement on a no-rip video: all 97 labelled
frames have empty foreground ground truth. In its candidate region, the two
model masks disagree on 100% of pixels, with a mean absolute probability
difference of 0.81125. The highest-disagreement frame list in the JSON includes
frames 6, 12, 18, 24, and 30 from this video. The candidate region here is
generated by model-positive pixels; it is not an independent annotation of
rip-current presence.

## Limits and Decision

The validation split was already used to select both model checkpoints.
These are **descriptive validation diagnostics**, not independent estimates
for unseen beaches. The candidate region uses ground truth and both model
predictions, so its scores are diagnostic and cannot be compared directly with
all-pixel scores as if sampled from the same population. ECE depends on the
predeclared binning and is not a guarantee of per-case reliability. No
temperature scaling, weight tuning, or external labelled-test evaluation was
performed.

Retain SegFormer as the current fixed-threshold segmentation reference and the
equal-weight ensemble as a useful probability-fusion comparator. Do not change
the default model or claim calibrated deployment confidence based on this run
alone. Any fitted correction requires a separately declared video-disjoint
assessment protocol; ideally it would also be checked on genuinely new labelled
videos.
